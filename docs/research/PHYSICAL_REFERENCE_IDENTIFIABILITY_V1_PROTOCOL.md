# Physical posture-reference identifiability pilot v1

Status: execution-ready proposed data-collection protocol; no participants recruited and
no measurements or outcomes claimed.

This protocol addresses the question that InclusiveHAR cannot answer: whether a local
posture reference is stable across separate bouts, visits, and sensor attachments. It is
a characterization study, not promotion or continuation of prompted-posture v1, whose
gate failed and remains closed.

Recruit six new development wearers for two visits. At each visit create two documented
attachment blocks by fully removing and reattaching the device according to a fixed
placement instruction, giving 24 attachment blocks. Within every block collect at least
three physically separate bouts each of sitting, standing, and mobility in a
counterbalanced order fixed before recruitment. Record raw inertial channels, timestamps,
units, axis convention, device identity, placement, attachment event, participant,
visit, block, trial, bout, prompt, acquisition failure, and sample-validity provenance.

Support bouts and evaluation bouts must be different physical bouts. No raw sample may
cross those roles. Seal evaluation labels until all characterization computations are
frozen. Participant is the independent unit; bouts, blocks, attachments, and visits do
not increase participant N. Six people provide a feasibility measurement and cannot
establish population superiority.

Before collection, freeze angular within-posture spread, sitting-standing separation,
cross-bout shift, cross-attachment shift, cross-visit shift, invalid-support rate, and
missingness summaries. Compare fresh support in the same block, stale support from the
other attachment in the visit, and stale support from the prior visit. Report every
person and failure. Do not choose margins, supports, or exclusions after labels.

The decision is informational. Stable geometry under observable scope may justify a new
architecture protocol with base-only, conventional angular prototype, agreement-gated,
and uncertainty-aware prototype controls at an identical label budget. A requirement for
fresh support becomes an explicit application burden. Geometry that is unstable even
with fresh support, or an attachment state that cannot be observed, closes this reference
route rather than motivating another router. Human recruitment, consent, ethics, device
operation and actual collection require their own lawful operational authorization and
are not performed by this repository change.
